# El Bloque — Story Bible (draft v1)

Working doc for lore, cast and the content plan. Nothing here is in the game yet unless it says **(in game)**. Items marked **⚑ default** are calls I made where we haven't decided yet — override freely.

---

## 1. Decisions so far

| Topic | Decision |
|---|---|
| Tone | **Warm + real.** Funny, affectionate, very boricua day-to-day; the street has real weight and consequences. |
| Life span | **Newborn → old age.** Childhood in the caserío, adulthood across PR, then your heir continues. |
| Storylines | **All four**, released one after another as seasons: Family mystery, Your lane, Save the caserío, The hurricane. |
| Parents | **Randomized per life** from a set of backstories (§4). |
| Street | **Every caserío runs differently**: its own boss, rules and history (§5). |
| Real PR | **Real events and places** (María/Fiona, LUMA blackouts, Fiestas de la Calle, real towns and landmarks). **Fictional people and businesses.** |
| First focus | **Deepen El Bloque.** |
| ⚑ default: Season order | Family → Lane → Save the caserío → Hurricane. Family fits childhood with Abuela; the hurricane is the big late shake-up. |
| ⚑ default: Childhood | 0–11 plays as fast yearly event cards (BitLife style) that set stats and traits. The map opens at 12. The current game starts at 16. |
| ⚑ default: Street content | Real but not graphic. Drugs, guns, jail and death exist and matter. No gore, no glorifying, and people you love can be lost. |
| Abuela | **Players name her** on the character screen ("Doña ___", default Carmen). **She can die at any age**, including before 18. |
| Street bosses | The four in §5 are approved for now. |
| ⚑ default: Home first | Las Palmas de Oro (San Juan) gets fleshed out first. The other three get the same treatment after. |

## 2. Life stages

| Age | Stage | How it plays |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 | **Bebé / nene** | 3–4 cards per year: Abuela's choices, first words, getting sick, a blackout birthday. Sets the hidden seeds for your don. |
| 6–11 | **Escuela elemental** | Cards plus tiny choices: fights at recess, Little League, a teacher who notices you, the first time you see el punto. |
| 12–15 | **Intermedia** | **The map unlocks**, in a kid version of El Bloque: mandados, the cancha, the colmado, no punto (yet). Season 1 starts here. |
| 16–17 | **Superior** | **The current game.** Full bloque, jobs, romance, the street opens. |
| 18–29 | **Joven adulto** | Universidad / trabajo / la calle. El pueblo and San Juan. Moving out (or not). |
| 30–59 | **Adulto** | Family, career peak, your own kids, power in the caserío. |
| 60+ | **Viejo/a** | Legacy, health, grandkids, playing dominó at Don Pepe's. You become someone's Abuelo/a. |
| Death | **Heir** | Your hijo/a takes over **(heirs exist in game)**, inheriting money, respect and enemies. |

## 3. The four seasons

### Season 1 — *La bendición* (Family mystery) · ages 12–17 **(in game, beats 1–5)**
Why you live with Abuela. Each life rolls one backstory (§4); the season's quests change to match it, but the beats are shared:
1. **Something doesn't add up**: a photo, a letter, a name nobody says.
2. **Abuela deflects**: she changes the subject and sends you on a mandado.
3. **The bloque knows**: Don Pepe, Doña Lydia and Los del 3 each hold a piece.
4. **The truth**: told by Abuela when you've earned it (high Afecto), or found out the hard way.
5. **What you do with it** sets up Season 2: forgive, confront, go looking, or get even.

### Season 2 — *Tu carril* (Your lane) · ages 16–29
A multi-chapter arc per lane (pelotero, rapero/artista, estudioso, buscón/negocio, la calle). Yadiel runs a parallel arc on a different lane, and your two paths cross.

### Season 3 — *El bloque no se vende* (Save the caserío) · ages 25+
A developer plus the Gobierno announce a "remodelación" of your residencial that would displace half the families. Every lane can fight it, profit from it or sell out: lawyer, artist with a platform, street money, politics. The outcome permanently changes the map.

### Season 4 — *Después del agua* (The hurricane)
A category-4 storm hits. Months without power, Abuela's health, people leaving for the States. Rebuilding is its own phase, with new places, closed places and who stayed.

## 5. Each caserío runs differently

Every caserío has its own street boss, history and "rules of the bloque". These are fictional people, but real-feeling.

### Res. Las Palmas de Oro · San Juan ⚑ home first
- **History:** Built in the '60s by the Expreso. The Hato Rey towers watch it like a judge. Proud, crowded, always busy.
- **The street:** **"El Gringo"** (Ángel Pérez, ~45, nicknamed for his green eyes). Old school: pays for the Reyes toys, the summer camp and funerals. No selling to kids, no robbing the bloque. Ruthless with anyone who breaks the rules.
- **The tension:** Los del 3 answer to El Gringo, but his own nephew wants him out.

### Res. Ceiba Alta · Ponce
- **History:** Wide streets, ponceño pride, the best court in the south.
- **The street:** **No single boss.** Three families split it by building with a truce from the '90s. The truce is cracking.

### Res. Arena Negra · Loíza
- **History:** Five minutes from the beach. Bomba on Fridays, frituras every day, deep Afro-boricua roots.
- **The street:** **"La Doña"** (Migdalia, ~60) runs it like a business and keeps it quiet. She doesn't want trouble in Loíza, she wants money in Loíza.

### Res. Quebrada Fría · Bayamón
- **History:** By the Tren Urbano and the stadium. The baseball route.
- **The street:** **Young and hungry.** **"Bebo"** (22) took over after the last boss was arrested. Unpredictable and flashy, and he wants a famous pelotero from his caserío on his side.

## 6. Expanded cast (El Bloque · Las Palmas de Oro)

### Abuela — Doña ___ (named by the player; default Carmen)
- 71. Retired school-cafeteria cook. Diabetic, refuses to admit it. Watches the novela at 8 and calls during it to tell you who betrayed who.
- Goes to the Pentecostal church on Tuesdays and Sundays. Keeps a shoebox under the bed **(Season 1 key)**.
- **Mechanic (in game):** **Afecto** and **Salud** bars in the Cel → Familia. Her Salud drops a little each night; health events (mareos, the pastillas, the hospital) let you take care of her. At 0 she dies. Season 1's truth needs 40 Afecto, or comes from her letter after she's gone.
- **Planned:** High Afecto unlocks the truth and her recipes (cooking buffs). Low Afecto means she stops covering for you.
- **Arc:** her health gets worse with age. Taking care of her (or not) is a real choice. Her death is a major life event; it can happen during the hurricane.

### Yadiel — your primo
- Same age, son of Abuela's other daughter (Titi Wanda). Better at baseball than you, worse at school. Lives in Edif. 5.
- **Arc:** his lane is rolled to differ from yours. Scout vs. the punto is the classic split. If he goes street, you can pull him out, join him or watch him fall. If he goes pro, he can bring you up or forget you.
- **Mechanic:** **Afecto**, plus **his own stats** that change over time based on your interactions.

### Chago (Santiago Ortiz) and Wichi — the found family
- **Chago**, 50s. Taller owner. Did 3 years when he was young and is very anti-street now. The legit-hustle mentor: teaches you mechanics, lends money and pays fair.
- **Wichi** (Luis), 19. Don Pepe's nephew. Works the colmado and knows everybody. Dreams of opening his own food truck.
- **Arc:** "El negocio". Wichi's food truck plus Chago's taller can become your first business (buscón lane). El Gringo's people come asking for "their cut". Do you pay?

### Los del 3 — the rival crew (named)
- **Kiko** **(in game, "el pana del 3")**, 17. Your way in. Funny, loyal, stuck. Could be your best friend or your first loss.
- **Toño "Mudo"**, 20. Barely talks. Runs the esquina for El Gringo's nephew.
- **Yeyo**, 24. The 3's shot-caller, ambitious, bad blood with the 7 over a cousin's death years ago.
- **Why the rivalry:** in 2015 a kid from the 7 and a kid from the 3 died the same night. Both buildings blame the other.
- **Paths:** take their esquina **(in game, as war)**, broker peace through Kiko (new), or stay out of it.

### Other bloque people (new)
- **Doña Lydia** **(in game)**: Panadería La Esperanza. Her son is in the Army in Texas. Knows Abuela's secret (Season 1).
- **Don Pepe** **(in game)**: the colmado. Dominó king. Has seen four bosses come and go. The bloque's memory.
- **Pastor Ramón**: Abuela's church. Runs the after-school program. Wants to save you from yourself.
- **Mrs. Cintrón**: English teacher at the Escuela Superior. "La maestra dice que tienes potencial" **(event in game)** becomes her.
- **Agente Rosado**: Policía Municipal. Grew up in the 5. Plays both sides, or none.
- **Titi Wanda**: Yadiel's mom, Abuela's daughter. Works double shifts at the hospital. Brutally honest.

## 9. Economy **(in game)**
- **Ánimo máximo**: 5, +1 each for Colchón nuevo, Tenis nuevos, Premium pass and Salud 80+. Café at Don Pepe's: +1 Ánimo once a day. Abanico: wake up with +1 extra.
- **Pase de las Fiestas de la Calle**: XP = Ánimo spent (8 per level), 10 levels, free and premium rewards you claim in the Tienda.
- **Coquís**: earned from story missions, birthdays and the pass. Spent on upgrades, Café grande, Abuela's gifts and medicine, and an abogado (−20 Calor).

## 10. Travel **(in game)**

**Viajar** (4th tab on the map, or *Coger la guagua* at La parada / *Coger un público* at the Terminal) opens a list of pueblos. Each trip costs the fare and 1 Ánimo; the first ride to each pueblo has its own story card with a choice. Going back to El bloque is free.

| Pueblo | How | Places |
|---|---|---|
| Bayamón | Guagua · $2 | Cárcel de Bayamón (visit Papi), El coliseo (BSN, tryouts), La chicharronera, Plaza Río Hondo (buy tenis with $) |
| Loíza | Guagua · $3 | Kioskos de Piñones, La playa (surf), Escuela de bomba, Taller de caretas |
| Arecibo | Público · $8 (after Capítulo 1) | Hospital de Arecibo, El Observatorio (post-2020), Cueva del Indio, El malecón |
| Utuado | Público · $10 | Casa de Abuela Toña (secreto only), Lago Caonillas, Río Tanamá, Hacienda cafetalera |
| Ponce | Público · $12 | Plaza Las Delicias, Castillo Serrallés, La Guancha, Res. Ceiba Alta |

Side-story trips (Papi in Bayamón, the Arecibo records, Abuela Toña in Utuado) happen at the real place.
